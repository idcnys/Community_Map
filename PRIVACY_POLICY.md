# Community Map Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-11

This Privacy Policy explains how Community Map ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Community Map app ("App").

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

### Account Information
- Email address
- Authentication identifiers (including third-party sign-in identifiers)
- Profile details you provide (such as name, bio, and contact info)

### Community and Report Content
- Posts, comments, likes, reposts, poll responses, and group interactions
- Reports and related details (including urgency, description, and status)
- Uploaded media such as images and audio recordings

### Location Information
- Device location data you allow the App to access
- Location data associated with reports, map activity, and optional group location sharing

### Device and Usage Information
- App interaction metadata (for example, feature usage and notification activity)
- Basic technical information required for security and reliability

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:
- create and manage your account;
- provide map, feed, reporting, chat, notification, and group features;
- show relevant content and coordinate community safety features;
- detect misuse, protect users, and improve app performance;
- comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Bases (Where Applicable)

Where required by law, we process data based on one or more of the following:
- your consent (for example, location and certain notifications);
- performance of a contract (providing core app functionality);
- legitimate interests (security, moderation, product improvement);
- compliance with legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing of Information

We may share information:
- with other users, based on feature context (for example, public posts, group posts, shared reports, and group location sharing you enable);
- with service providers that help operate the App (such as cloud hosting, authentication, messaging, storage, and analytics providers);
- when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell personal information for direct monetary compensation.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party platforms and SDKs, including services for authentication, cloud database/storage, messaging, media, and maps. Those providers process information under their own policies and terms.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the App, support community features, resolve disputes, enforce policies, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirements.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You can:
- update certain profile information in the App;
- manage device permissions (such as location and notifications) in your device settings;
- request account or data actions according to applicable law.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port your personal data.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. If you believe a child submitted personal data in violation of law, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 10. International Data Transfers

If data is processed in countries other than your own, we take steps intended to provide appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after updates take effect means you accept the revised policy.

For privacy questions or requests, add and maintain a valid privacy contact email or support channel in this section.
