📥 Download Guide — Which APK do I need?

Please download the version matching your device architecture for optimal app size and performance:

  • Cmap-arm64-v8a.apk (Recommended)
    For: Most modern Android smartphones & tablets (64-bit ARM).
  • Cmap-release.apk (Universal)
    For: Any device. Download this if you're unsure which version your device supports.
  • Cmap-armeabi-v7a.apk
    For: Older or low-end 32-bit Android devices.
  • Cmap-x86_64.apk
    For: PC emulators (BlueStacks, LDPlayer), Android Studio emulator, or x86 Chromebooks/tablets.
